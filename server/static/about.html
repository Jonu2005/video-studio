🎬 Video Studio ke baare mein / About Video Studio

Hindi: Video Studio ek muft video banane wala tool hai. Aap apni script likhte hain, aur tool usse ek documentary-style video bana deta hai — Hindi ya English voiceover, har ~7 second mein badalne wale visuals (asli stock video footage aur AI images), on-screen text, captions aur music ke saath. Video banana aur download karna abhi bilkul muft hai: roz 3 videos tak bana sakte hain, poori Full HD (1080p) video ke saath.

English: Video Studio is a free video-making tool. You write a script and the tool turns it into a documentary-style video — Hindi or English narration, visuals that change about every 7 seconds (real stock video footage and AI images), on-screen text, captions and music. Making and downloading videos is completely free right now: up to 3 videos per day, in full Full HD (1080p).

Video kaise banti hai / How videos are made

  • Voiceover ek free open-source text-to-speech voice se banta hai (Piper). Awaaz computer se bani hoti hai, kisi insaan ki recording nahi.
  • Visuals free stock video libraries (Pexels, Pixabay, Wikimedia Commons) se aate hain, aur jahan footage nahi milti wahan AI se banayi gayi images istemaal hoti hain. AI images par kabhi-kabhi galtiyaan ho sakti hain (galat subject, ajeeb details).
  • Music ek simple computer-generated background track hai.
  • Video banne mein time lagta hai: 2–3 minute ki video ke liye free hosting par lagbhag 5–10 minute.
