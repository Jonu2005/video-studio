Refund Policy — Video Studio

Last updated: 22 November 2026.

Refund kab milega / When you get a refund

  • Payment ho gaya lekin download nahi khula: agar ₹1 pay karne ke baad bhi Full HD download nahi ho raha hai (technical problem), to poora ₹1 refund milega.
  • Download ki hui video kharaab hai: agar downloaded video file corrupt hai ya chal nahi rahi, to refund milega — ya pehle hum video dobara banane ki koshish karenge.
  • Galti se do baar payment ho gaya: ek hi video ke liye do baar pay ho gaya ho to doosra ₹1 refund milega.

Refund kab nahi milega / When you do not get a refund

  • Quality pasand nahi aayi: har video ka 30 second ka preview pay karne se PEHLE muft dikhaya jata hai. Preview dekh kar pay karne ke baad "video achhi nahi lagi" par refund nahi milega.
  • Download ho gaya aur video chal rahi hai: digital download successful hone ke baad refund nahi hota.
  • Galat video ke liye pay kar diya: kripya pay karne se pehle video ka title aur preview check karein.

Refund kaise maangein / How to ask for a refund

[SUPPORT_EMAIL] par email karein — apna Google account email, video ka title, aur payment ki date likhein. Hum 7 din ke andar jawab denge. Refund approve hone par paise usi UPI/Card account mein wapas jayenge jis se payment hua tha (Razorpay ke through, 5–7 working days lag sakte hain).

Free service / Muft seva

Video banana aur preview dekhna bilkul muft hai — iske liye koi payment nahi hota, isliye is par refund ka sawaal hi nahi uthta.
